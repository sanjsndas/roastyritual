User Rights and Compliance with the CCPA

We acknowledge and uphold your entitlements concerning the personal information we gather, process, and retain. Pursuant to the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), qualified individuals possess distinct rights related to their data.

These entitlements encompass, but are not confined to:

  • Right of Access: You are entitled to solicit details regarding the types and specific elements of personal data we accumulate, alongside clarification on the intentions behind its usage.
  • Right to Erasure: You possess the prerogative to demand the permanent removal of any personal data we have collected. We will fulfill such deletion requests in accordance with prevailing legal mandates, excepting any statutory or regulatory obligations that necessitate data retention.
  • Right to Decline: You hold the choice to opt-out of the sale or dissemination of your personal information to third parties for purposes not integral to our website's operation.
  • Right to Non-Bias: You are assured the right to exercise these privileges without incurring any detrimental effects on the services and functionalities we offer.

To invoke these rights, kindly submit your request via the specific contact methods provided on our website. Be aware that identity verification may be required in certain instances before we process your request, serving to protect the security and integrity of your data.

Our firm commitment is to uphold these rights and consistently apply the utmost levels of transparency in all our data processing activities.
